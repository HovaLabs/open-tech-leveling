Straightforward Career Leveling for Software Engineers

Explore the different levels of software engineering roles from L1 to L8 in simple terms with no gatekeeping or fluffy jargon.

This leveling system originates from Hova Labs' work with startups, enterprise, and everything in between.

Software engineering career levels L1 to L8, described across engineering excellence, software design and architecture, impact scope, level of ambiguity, ownership, and team building
Level Title Engineering Excellence Software Design + Architecture Impact Scope Level of Ambiguity Ownership Team Building
L1 Junior Engineer Basic coding skills, learning best practices, follows coding standards with guidance Works on well-defined components, implements existing patterns Individual tasks, small features Clear requirements, well-defined problems Executes ticket-level work with guidance. Plans within sprint goals and builds context on how their work fits in. Learns from team, asks questions, participates in team activities
L2 Software Engineer Proficient in primary tech stack, writes clean, testable code independently Designs simple systems, understands common patterns and anti-patterns Full features, multiple related tasks Some ambiguity, can break down medium-sized problems Independently owns and delivers features. Plans across sprints and contributes to scoping and estimations. Collaborates effectively, provides constructive code reviews
L3 Senior Engineer Expert in multiple technologies, champions best practices, optimizes for performance Designs complex systems, makes architectural decisions for team-level projects Team-level impact, cross-functional collaboration Comfortable with ambiguous requirements, defines technical approach Owns major systems or components. Plans across quarters, aligning team execution with roadmap goals. Mentors junior engineers, leads technical discussions
L4 Staff Engineer Deep expertise, introduces new technologies, establishes engineering standards Architects multi-team systems, ensures scalability and maintainability Multi-team initiatives, cross-functional impact Thrives in ambiguous environments, defines problems and solutions Leads initiatives across multiple teams. Aligns technical strategy with product direction across quarters. Develops team technical capabilities, influences engineering culture
L5 Senior Staff Engineer Industry-level expertise, drives technical vision, evaluates emerging technologies Defines architecture for major platform initiatives, ensures long-term scalability Department-level impact, influences product strategy Navigates high ambiguity, creates clarity from complex business needs Owns platform-critical systems. Frequently asked questions

What is Open Tech Leveling?

Open Tech Leveling is a free, open-source career leveling framework for software engineers. It defines eight levels, from L1 Junior Engineer to L8 Fellow, and describes each one across six dimensions: Engineering Excellence, Software Design + Architecture, Impact Scope, Level of Ambiguity, Ownership, and Team Building. It was created by Hova Labs and is MIT-licensed on GitHub.

What are the software engineering levels from L1 to L8?

The eight levels are L1 Junior Engineer, L2 Software Engineer, L3 Senior Engineer, L4 Staff Engineer, L5 Senior Staff Engineer, L6 Principal Engineer, L7 Distinguished Engineer, and L8 Fellow. Each step up widens an engineer's scope, from individual tasks at L1 to team-level impact at L3, organization-wide impact at L6, and global impact at L8.

What is the difference between a Senior Engineer and a Staff Engineer?

The main difference is scope. A Senior Engineer (L3) has team-level impact: they design complex systems, make architectural decisions for team-level projects, and mentor junior engineers. A Staff Engineer (L4) works across multiple teams: they architect multi-team systems, lead initiatives across teams, define problems as well as solutions, and influence engineering culture.

What is the difference between a Staff Engineer and a Principal Engineer?

A Staff Engineer (L4) leads initiatives across multiple teams and plans across quarters. A Principal Engineer (L6) owns organization-wide architecture and technical direction, leads multi-year roadmaps that support company strategy, and develops technical leaders. The Senior Staff Engineer level (L5) sits between them, with department-level impact and a 6–12 month planning horizon.

How are software engineering levels measured?

Open Tech Leveling measures each level across six dimensions: Engineering Excellence (technical skill and craft), Software Design + Architecture (the complexity of systems an engineer designs), Impact Scope (how far their work reaches), Level of Ambiguity (how well-defined their problems are), Ownership (what they own and how far ahead they plan), and Team Building (how they grow the people around them).

How do I get promoted to the next engineering level?

Compare your current work with the next level's description in each of the six dimensions. The gaps show where to grow, whether that means taking on more ambiguous problems, owning larger systems, planning further ahead, or mentoring others. Managers can use the same descriptions to set clear, shared expectations for promotion.

Can my company use Open Tech Leveling?

Yes. Open Tech Leveling is free and released under the MIT License, so any company or team can use, adapt, or extend it. The framework lives on GitHub at github.com/HovaLabs/open-tech-leveling, where contributions are welcome.
